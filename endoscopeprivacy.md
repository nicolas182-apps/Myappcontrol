Privacy Policy for USB Endoscope: Borescope & OTG
Last Updated: February 9, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how USB Endoscope: Borescope & OTG (referred to as "the App") handles your information.

1. No Data Collection
The App is designed to be a purely offline utility.

No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide any login credentials to use the App.
No Internet Access: The App does not have permission to access the internet; therefore, no data can be transmitted from your device to our servers or any third-party services.
2. Permissions and Usage
The App requires certain permissions to function correctly. These permissions are used exclusively for local operations:

Camera: Required to access and display the live video feed from your connected USB endoscope, borescope, or external camera.
Microphone (Audio): Optional. Used only if you choose to record video with sound. Audio data is processed locally and never shared.
Storage (Photos/Media/Files): Required to save the photos you take and the videos you record directly to your device's local storage.
3. Data Storage
All photos and videos captured through the App are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to these files, and they are not uploaded to any cloud service by this App.

4. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

5. Children's Privacy
Since we do not collect any personal information, the App is safe for users of all ages and complies with privacy regulations regarding children.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
